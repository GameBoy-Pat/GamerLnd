THE GAP

A critic can influence your opinion. A trusted player can actually guide it.

  • A 7/10 from a major outlet might still be a 9/10 for you.
  • Consensus is broad. Compatibility is personal.
  • The right recommendation usually comes from players who think like you.

WHAT MAKES GAMERLND DIFFERENT

Built for player-driven discovery.

  • Built around real player opinions—not critic scores
  • Discovery based on alignment, not averages
  • Designed to be used daily—not just checked occasionally

THE IDEA

GamerLnd is built around two beliefs.

01

Game discovery should come from real players—not just review scores.

Big scores flatten everything into a number. GamerLnd helps you follow players whose opinions actually line up with yours, so recommendations feel relevant instead of random.

02

The app itself should feel rewarding to use.

GamerLnd is not just a tracker. GL, Milestones, Challenges, and Secrets give the product momentum, surprise, and a layer of progression that makes it feel alive.
