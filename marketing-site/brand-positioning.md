## GamerLnd Positioning

GamerLnd is a player-first game discovery and logging app built around a simple belief:

The best game recommendations come from people who actually know your taste.

### The problem

Game discovery is often shaped by critic scores, outlet reviews, and broad consensus. That can be useful context, but it is not personal. A critic may be respected, but they still do not know what kinds of games hit hardest for you.

Consensus is broad. Compatibility is personal.

### The shift GamerLnd makes

GamerLnd moves discovery away from critic-first authority and closer to trusted player opinion.

Instead of asking only, "What score did this get?"

GamerLnd asks:

- Who is recommending this?
- Do they share my taste?
- Have they liked the same kinds of games I like?
- Do I trust how they talk about games?

### What GamerLnd offers

- Trusted discovery through real players
- A place to log, rate, review, rank, and remember games
- Lists and profiles that make taste visible
- A fun progression layer through GL, Milestones, Challenges, and Secrets

### Brand language

Preferred phrases:

- player-first discovery
- trusted discovery
- real players over consensus
- people who share your taste
- compatibility is personal
- better discovery starts with better people

Avoid:

- bitter anti-critic language
- generic "social gaming" phrasing
- treating gamification as the core product

### One-paragraph version

GamerLnd helps players discover games through people they actually trust. While critics and broad averages can shape the conversation, they do not know your taste. GamerLnd gives players a better way: follow trusted people, discover through shared taste, and build a personal history through ratings, reviews, rankings, lists, and game logs.
